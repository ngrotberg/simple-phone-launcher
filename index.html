Why does an app open its website instead of the app?

Apps under "Opens straight from the widget" open directly only when they are installed. If one is installed and still opens the website, iOS may be remembering an earlier choice to open that site in Safari. Touch and hold a link to that site in Safari and choose to open it in the app, or reinstall the app.

Why does Shortcuts appear when I open Phone or Messages?

Apple's own apps have no link another app can use to open them. Simple opens them through an Apple Shortcut, and iOS shows Shortcuts for a moment. This is an iOS limit that applies to every launcher.

My widget shows old names.

iOS decides when widgets refresh. Open Simple, go to Edit, and tap Refresh widgets. If a widget still shows old names, remove it and add it again.

How do I make the Home Screen only show Simple?

Open Simple and tap Setup. The guide covers the widget, a matching wallpaper, a Focus that shows only that page, and quieting notifications.
