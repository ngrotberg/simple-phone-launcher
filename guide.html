What you end up with

A Home Screen that is a short list of words. Up to eight app names in plain text on a plain background. Tap a name and the app opens. No icons, no badges, no colour, no second page. App Library and Search are still there when you need something else.

What iOS allows

iOS has no launcher replacement, so every "dumb phone" setup on iPhone is the same three parts: a widget, a matching wallpaper, and a Focus that shows only one page. Simple supplies the widget and a guide for the rest. Apple's own apps (Phone, Messages, Camera) have no link another app can open, so they go through a one-time Shortcut and Shortcuts flashes for a moment. That is true of every launcher on iOS.

Step 1. Pick your apps

  1. Install Simple and tap Choose apps.
  2. Apps under Opens straight from the widget open directly on iOS 18 and later. Each link is checked against the app's own published link file before release.
  3. Apple's apps ask you to create a one-time Shortcut with the Open App action, named exactly as shown. Turn on "I created this shortcut" and save.
  4. Stop at eight. That limit is the point.

Step 2. Add the widget

  1. Hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Simple.
  2. Quick access (medium) shows up to eight names in two columns. Essentials (large) shows them in one. On iOS 27, swipe to Essentials' largest size and it fills the whole page.
  3. Move every ordinary icon off this page and out of the Dock. They stay in App Library.

Step 3. Match the wallpaper

  1. In Simple, tap Setup and save the wallpaper. It is sized for your iPhone and matches your palette.
  2. Settings → Wallpaper: use it for the Home Screen, Blur off.
  3. On iOS 18 and later, hold the Home Screen → Edit → Customize → Large to hide widget labels. Keep Default or Dark; Clear and Tinted draw widgets as glass.

Step 4. Hide the other pages

  1. Settings → Focus → create a custom Focus named Simple.
  2. Under Customize Screens, choose only this page. Choose who and what can notify you.
  3. Turning the Focus off in Control Center brings the other pages back. Nothing is deleted.

Step 5. Quiet the rest

  1. Settings → Home Screen & App Library → new downloads to App Library only.
  2. Turn off the notification badges you don't need.
  3. Optional: Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Color Filters → Grayscale.
