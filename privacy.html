Privacy Policy

Simple Phone Launcher · Effective September 12, 2026

Simple collects nothing. There is no account, analytics, advertising, tracking, or server. Nothing you do in Simple is sent to the developer or anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

The app names, links, shortcut names, and appearance you choose are stored on your iPhone, in storage shared only with Simple's own widgets. Deleting Simple deletes them.

A backup you export is a file you control. Simple never uploads it.

When you open an app

Tapping a name hands its link or shortcut to iOS, which opens that app, Apple Shortcuts, or your browser. Their own privacy policies apply from there.

Children

Simple collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue, or see the support page.
